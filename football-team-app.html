What your coaches get

Who's comingPlayers reply to each of your training sessions and matches with one tap, so you know numbers before you arrive. Take the register in seconds.
Ratings and progressRate players after each session and their form chart builds itself across the season.
Food and trainingPlayers log meals (or snap a photo) and workouts, and you see the whole squad at a glance.
Monthly reportsA report for each player with attendance, ratings, training and a coach's note, ready to print or send to parents.
WhatsApp and calendarShare sessions to your team's WhatsApp group and add the schedule to any phone calendar.
English and ArabicThe whole app works in both languages, on any phone or computer, with nothing to download.

Made for football

When you pick football for your club, the app uses the right words and numbers: training sessions and matches, positions from goalkeeper to striker, and season stats for appearances, goals, assists and minutes.

Line-ups for match day

Build your starting XI on a pitch with 4-3-3, 4-4-2, 4-2-3-1 and 3-5-2, or 7-a-side and 5-a-side for younger age groups. Add your subs and share it to the team's WhatsApp group in one tap. Players who are starting see it on their home screen.
