Monthly reportsA report for each player with attendance, ratings, training and a coach's note, ready to print or send to parents.
WhatsApp and calendarShare sessions to your team's WhatsApp group and add the schedule to any phone calendar.
English and ArabicThe whole app works in both languages, on any phone or computer, with nothing to download.

Made for volleyball

When you pick volleyball for your club, the app uses the right words and numbers: training sessions and matches, positions from setter, outside hitter, opposite, middle blocker and libero, and season stats for matches, kills, blocks and aces.

Line-ups for match day

Place your six on a court diagram, add subs, and send the line-up to the team in one tap.
