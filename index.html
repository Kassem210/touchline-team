Features

Everything a coach checks before training, in one screen

No more chasing players on group chats or keeping spreadsheets. Players log in seconds, and coaches see the whole squad live.

Food tracking with photo scan

Players snap their plate or describe the meal, and AI works out calories, protein, carbs and fat. They can correct the numbers before saving. Coaches set daily targets per player.

Registers and form

Take the register in a few taps after each session and add a 1–10 rating if you want. Attendance rates and a form chart build up for every player across the season.

Plans, feedback and instructions

Send a player a training plan as a checklist, or feedback after a match. Instructions need a “Got it” from the player, so you know they’ve read them.

Availability before every session

Players say “I’ll be there” or “Can’t make it” with a reason. You see who’s coming before you plan the session, and absences arrive already marked as excused.

Match-day squads and notices

Post the squad for Saturday or a kit reminder to the team board. Selected players see a “You’re in the squad” banner as soon as they open the app.

Alerts that matter

The coach’s Today screen flags who has missed sessions, who can’t make the next one, who is well under their protein target, and who hasn’t read your instructions.

Privacy and safety

Built for young athletes from day one

Food diaries and ratings are personal. Touchline Team keeps them between each player and their coaches.

Players only see their own data

Teammates can’t see each other’s food, ratings, plans or feedback. This is enforced on the server, not just hidden on screen.

Parent or guardian consent for under-16s

Younger players can only join with a parent or guardian’s agreement, and AI food scanning is switched off for them.

Only coaches set ratings and stats

Players can’t edit their own ratings, attendance or targets. Only the club owner decides who is a coach.

Download or delete any time

Anyone can download everything stored about them or delete their account. Club owners can delete a club and all its data.

No ads, no selling data

We don’t show ads and we never sell or share your data for marketing. Meal photos aren’t stored.

FAQ

Questions coaches ask

Do players need to download an app?

No. It works in any up-to-date browser on a phone, tablet or computer. On a phone, players can use “Add to Home Screen” so it opens like an app.

Which sports does it work for?

Any team or individual sport. Football, basketball, tennis and gymnastics have their own season stats and wording (like “Match” or “Meet”), and there’s a general option for everything else.

How accurate is the photo food scanning?

It gives a good estimate for everyday meals, but it isn’t exact. Portions are hard to judge from a photo. Players see what the scanner found and can adjust the numbers before saving, and they can always type the numbers themselves. It’s a tool for building good habits, not medical or dietary advice.

Can players see each other’s data?

No. Teammates see each other’s names, shirt numbers and squad selections, but food logs, ratings, plans and feedback are only visible to the player and their coaches.

We have players under 16. Is that OK?

Yes. Under-16s need a parent or guardian to agree when they sign up, and AI food scanning is switched off for their accounts. Coaches confirm they’ll tell players and parents what the app records.

Can I have more than one coach?

Yes. Assistant coaches join with the same club code, and the club owner makes them a coach. Coaches see the whole squad. Only the owner can change roles or delete the club.

What happens to our data if we stop using it?

The club owner can delete the club and everything in it at any time. Players can download their own data or delete their accounts whenever they like.

What does it cost?

$20 a month per club, with unlimited players and coaches. Every new club gets a 30-day free trial with no card needed. The club owner pays, players and parents never do.

What happens if we stop paying?

Your club becomes read-only. Everyone can still see and download their data, and nothing is deleted. Subscribe again and everything carries on where you left off.
